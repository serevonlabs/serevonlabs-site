Serevon Labs Website - v6.1 Visual Refinements
Date: 2026-10-04

Changes in this revision:
- Reworked the About-page hero illustration so the Business Software panel now shows four concrete delivery stages instead of generic placeholder lines.
- Replaced the Systems dot/orbit placeholder with an integration map showing API, ERP, CRM, data, and cloud connectivity.
- Expanded the Platforms panel to explicitly show Web, Android, iOS, and Windows.
- Enlarged the Web Apps and Enterprise cards in the Business Software Solutions illustration and made them the same size.
- Reflowed the Web Apps and Enterprise descriptions onto two lines so no copy runs outside the cards.
- Centered Plan, Design, Build, Integrate, and Support inside the dark workflow bar at the bottom of the illustration.
- Moved the workflow labels into the SVG itself so their alignment remains correct at different screen sizes.
- Changed capability illustrations to preserve their native aspect ratio instead of cropping with object-fit: cover.
- Centered the capability tags over the dark footer band on the About-page capability illustration.
- Added a mid-size desktop breakpoint for the About hero to prevent the two-column artwork from becoming cramped before the mobile layout takes over.

No ContinuCam legal content or application functionality was changed in this revision.
