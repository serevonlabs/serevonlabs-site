# Optional Adobe Stock media for SerevonLabs.com

The v6 site does **not** depend on unlicensed stock imagery. The redesign uses responsive interface artwork, gradients, and the site's existing product/illustration assets so it is ready to publish as-is.

If you want to add licensed photography later, the strongest placement is the About page, where a real modern team/workspace image can add warmth without turning the homepage into a generic stock-photo site.

Recommended Adobe Stock directions:

1. **Modern software / technology team**
   - Search: `business technology team` or `software development team modern office`
   - Good for: About page / company story section
   - Adobe Stock search: https://stock.adobe.com/search/images?filters%5Bcontent_type%3Aphoto%5D=1&hide_panel=true&k=business+technology+team

2. **Digital transformation / business collaboration**
   - Search: `digital transformation team`
   - Good for: a future case-study or services banner
   - Adobe Stock search: https://stock.adobe.com/search?ca=0&k=digital+transformation+team

3. **Abstract business technology / network background**
   - Search: `abstract business technology`
   - Good for: optional subtle full-width background imagery or video
   - Adobe Stock search: https://stock.adobe.com/search?k=abstract+business+technology

4. **Business analytics / dashboard imagery**
   - Search: `business analytics dashboard`
   - Good for: enterprise-services page or future case studies
   - Adobe Stock search: https://stock.adobe.com/search/images?k=business+analytics+dashboard

## Recommended selection style

Prefer imagery that is:
- Dark or neutral enough to work with the Serevon blue/navy palette.
- Wide (16:9 or wider) with usable negative space.
- Natural and credible rather than heavily staged.
- Focused on collaboration, workflows, or technology in context rather than generic close-ups of code.

After purchasing an image, export a web-sized WebP around 1800–2400px wide and keep the original licensed file in your asset archive.
